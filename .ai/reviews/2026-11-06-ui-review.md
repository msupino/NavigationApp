# NavAid UI review findings

Review target: `9eda509` (the dev snapshot). This report records the completed UI review, the contrast correction, and the tablet layout choice added in this PR.

### [FIXED, MEDIUM] Light theme toolbar group captions were below readable contrast
- Where: desktop menubar dropdowns, `.tb-frame-title` captions in Extra layers and Display; EN and HE, light theme, 1280px and 820px.
- What: before the fix, the foreground was `rgb(138, 132, 132)` (`#8a8484`) at 3.58:1 on the light menu surface. The 10px captions were below the 4.5:1 target. The light theme now uses `#5b6672`, measuring 5.69:1 against the menu's `rgb(250, 252, 255)` surface.
- Evidence: matched before/after captures with identical app state: [English light Extra layers before](assets/ui-review-en-light-weather-before.png), [after](assets/ui-review-en-light-weather-after.png); [Hebrew light Display before](assets/ui-review-he-light-display-before.png), [after](assets/ui-review-he-light-display-after.png). Only the caption color differs in each pair.
- Enlarged comparison: [English caption before and after](assets/ui-review-caption-contrast-comparison.png).
- Why it matters / which rule: group labels help pilots scan and distinguish controls; sufficient contrast supports users with low vision.
- Fix: add a light-theme `.tb-frame-title` color override in `docs/app/style.css`; `tests/toolbar-caption-contrast.spec.js` verifies at least 4.5:1 in English and Hebrew.
