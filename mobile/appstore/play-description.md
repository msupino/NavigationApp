NavAid — CVFR flight planning & navigation for Israel

NavAid is a VFR / CVFR flight-planning and in-flight navigation aid built for Israeli airspace. Plan a route in seconds, read the chart the way you fly it, and keep the plan, the weather and the traffic in one place — on the ground and in the air.

Israel's CVFR system is built in: the published reporting points, frequency changes, altitude pairs and flight-plan filing rules match the AIP and the chart.

PLANNING
• Build a route by typing reporting-point codes (e.g. LLHZ BAZRA DEROR) or tapping the map
• Legs with headings, distances, altitudes, drift lines and ETE/ETA
• Magnetic variation from the World Magnetic Model, computed offline
• Printable flight plan and nav log; aircraft profiles (speed, fuel, climb/descent)
• Flight-plan filing helper that follows AIP Israel timing rules

THE CHART
• CVFR, Navigation, Low-Alt, Satellite and street base maps
• Airspace (TMA / CTR / restricted / prohibited) with limits, airfields, VORs, reporting points and frequency-change points
• Airfield approach and aerodrome charts, IFR chart, terrain and LSA bubbles

WEATHER & NOTAMs
• Live NOTAMs with decoded text and a look-ahead timeline
• SIGMET, AIRMET, winds/temps aloft and significant-weather charts
• A route check that flags closures, weather and blocked legs before you go

IN FLIGHT
• GPS position with automatic track recording
• Live traffic from ADS-B — other aircraft, with callsign and level
• Comm-failure guidance and the frequency table at a glance
• Follow me: share your live position over an encrypted link (optional)

OFFLINE & PRIVATE
• Download map tiles for offline use — works without a signal
• Your routes, positions and flight plans stay on your device; no account required. Optional Google Drive sync backs up your own settings to your own Drive
• No ads

LANGUAGES
• Full Hebrew and English, right-to-left throughout

Supports external Bluetooth GPS and a cockpit ADS-B receiver.

OFFICIAL DATA SOURCES
Aeronautical data in NavAid is derived from Israel's official publications. Always verify against the current official source:
• Charts, airspace, reporting points, frequencies and flight-plan rules — Aeronautical Information Publication (AIP), Civil Aviation Authority of Israel: https://www.gov.il/en/departments/guides/aip-israel
• NOTAMs — Israel Airports Authority, Aeronautical Information Service: https://www.iaa.gov.il/en/about/aeronautical-information/
• Aviation weather — Israel Meteorological Service: https://ims.gov.il

NavAid is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Civil Aviation Authority of Israel, the Israel Airports Authority, the Israel Meteorological Service, or any other government entity.

IMPORTANT: NavAid is a planning and situational-awareness aid, not a certified navigation system. Always fly with current official charts and NOTAMs and comply with ATC and the AIP. Do not use it as your sole means of navigation.
