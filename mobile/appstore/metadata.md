## Description

NavAid is a flight-planning aid for VFR pilots flying in Israeli airspace.

Draw a route on the official CVFR, ATS or Low Altitude chart and read the numbers a
navigation log asks for: magnetic heading, distance, time en route, cumulative time and
fuel. Terrain along the route is drawn as a profile, with minimum safe altitudes per leg.

• Current Israeli charts — CVFR, ATS, Low Altitude, helicopter routes, IFR overlays
• Navigation log and flight-plan form, ready to file
• NOTAM, airspace, METAR/TAF, wind and significant-weather charts on the chart itself
• Airfield plates, circuits and reporting points
• Live position, GPS track recording, and a terrain and airspace profile
• Follow me: share your position with someone on the ground, end-to-end encrypted
• Offline route planning and CVFR charts after the CVFR download reports ready
• Hebrew and English

NavAid is a planning aid. It is not certified for navigation and must not be used as a
primary reference in flight. Always plan and fly using current official charts, AIP, NOTAM
and weather, and comply with all applicable regulations and ATC instructions. The pilot in
command is responsible for the safe conduct of every flight.
